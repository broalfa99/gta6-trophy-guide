Roadmap to the Platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies · no mission replay, no New Game+. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual save slots).

Missable trophies

Only one trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — the Chapter 1 balcony meditation at Jason's house (see Stage 1). Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story. Story trophies (12) unlock automatically and cannot be missed.
